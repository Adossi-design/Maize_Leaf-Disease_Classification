How to take a good photo

  • Photograph one leaf, close up, so it fills the square.
  • Use daylight. Avoid deep shadow and direct sun on the lens.
  • Hold the phone still. Tap the screen to focus before shooting.
  • Choose a leaf that shows the spots or marks you are worried about.
  • Check several plants across the field, not one leaf.

What this tool can and cannot do

Knows
Four things only: Blight, Common Rust, Gray Leaf Spot and healthy maize leaves. It cannot recognise other crops, pests, or damage from drought or poor soil.
Accuracy
Right 93.0% of the time on the 629 test photos it never trained on. It learned mostly from close-up photos in good light, so photos taken in a field are less reliable.
Weak spot
Blight and Gray Leaf Spot look alike. In testing the model called 18 of the 86 Gray Leaf Spot leaves Blight, about 1 in 5.
Privacy
The check runs on your phone. Photos are not uploaded and no one else sees them.
No signal
Once the page has opened on this phone once, it keeps working without network, because the app and the model are stored on the phone.
Keys
P reads the leaf, C clears the photo, 1 2 3 switch sections.
Advice
A helper, not a diagnosis. Confirm with your local agricultural extension officer before spending money on treatment.
